Banaras Itinerary for 2 Days
Day 1
1. Start with Ganga Aarti at Assi ghat(Reach till 4:30)
2. Take a walk along the Ghats and stop Kedar Ghat.
Have the Darshan at Gauri Kedareshwar temple. The temple is more significant than Kedarnath
Dham.
3. Start again and stop at Dashashwamedh Ghat.
4. Reach Godowlia and have morning Banarasi nasta
Options: Uttapam at Godowalia, Choti Kachodi in front of Shapuri Mall (I prefer this), Neelu
Kachodi at Chowk, Bread makkhan at Laxmi tea(overpriced not overrated).
5. After Nasta you can visit Man Mahal Observatory at Man Mahal Ghat(near Dashshwamedh Ghat ).
6. Then you can visit Kaal Bhairav Temple(The protector of Kashi), which must be visited before Shri
Kashi Vishwanath Dham. Take permission from Baba for your stay in Banaras.
7. After the Darshan, you can roam around in the beautiful lanes of Kashi asking about Bramha Ghat
to the locals. ज्यादा मत घूमना खो जाओगे ।
8. On Bramha Ghat you'll find Kashi Dham Museum (closed on mondays, Open till 5pm)
9. After Kashi Dham, take a Ghat walk to the Rajendra Prasad Ghat and watch the most celebrated
Ganga Aarti.
10. Visit Godowlia and have some evening snack at any place lekin tum jaoge Kashi Chat Bhandar hi.
11. Then visit Annapurna Ji temple.
12. Attend Sayan Aarti at Shree Kashi Vishwanath ji. No ticket required.(Time: 10:15 pm).
Also आरती के बाद घंटी मत बजाना वरना सारे बनारसी लपेट लेंगे ।
13. सो जाओ एक दिन में पूरा बनारस घूमोगे क्या ?
Day 2
1. Take a boat ride from Dashashwamedh or Assi and enjoy the serenity of Ghats. (घर जाएंगा तेरा
इसमें )
2. Reach Lanka and witness the ever grand Banaras Hindu University (BHU). Also BHU वाले भूल
जाते है बनारस में BHU है BHU में बनारस नहीं तो याद दिलाते आना ।
3. Take blessings from Vishwanath Temple or VT of BHU established by Birla Family.

4. From there you can go to the Sankat Mochan temple (बैठ के हनुमान चालीसा पढ़ लेना time तो है ही
बहोत)
5. After that you can also visit Tulsi Manas Mandir, Durga Kund and Mani Mandir.
6. You can't get enough of Ganga Aarti in one watch. So you'll again watch Ganga Aarti.
7. Try Thandai at Godowlia. (रंगीन मतलब भांग, बाबा का प्रसाद नहीं है वो भंगेड़ी)
8. Pack your bags and go home. याद आएगी तुम्हे बनारस की ।
Best time to visit Banaras: November to February.

1. This city has everything but the real heritage of this city is its people.

3 Days in Varanasi
Save This Ultimate Itinerary — holidify
Day 1
Morning:
Start early at Dashashwamedh Ghat with a sunrise boat ride to watch the ghats come alive with
morning aarti.
Late Morning:
Wander through the narrow lanes of the Old City — visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple and Annapurna
Temple.
Stop for chai near Manikarnika Ghat to observe the city’s timeless rhythm.
Enjoy breakfast at Kachori Gali or Vishwanath Gali.
Lunch:
Try local street food at Kashi Chat Bhandar, or enjoy a lassi at Blue Lassi Shop.
Afternoon:
Explore Thatheri Bazaar for brassware, rudraksha beads, and Banarasi sweets.
Return to your hotel for some rest.
Evening:
Head back to Dashashwamedh Ghat for the iconic Ganga Aarti — best experienced from a boat.
Dinner:
Dine with river views at Dolphin Restaurant or Varuna (Hotel Clarks).
Day 2
Morning:
Begin at Assi Ghat for Subah-e-Banaras, the city’s morning music and yoga ritual.
Have breakfast or coffee at Open Hand Café.
Late Morning:
Visit nearby temples — Tulsi Manas Mandir, Durga Temple, and Sankat Mochan Hanuman
Temple, all within 10–15 minutes of each other.
Lunch:
Relax at Pizzeria Vaatika Café or Brown Bread Bakery overlooking Assi Ghat.
Try a Banarasi paan or lassi from Pahalwan afterward.
Afternoon:
Explore the Banaras Hindu University (BHU) campus and visit the Bharat Kala Bhavan Museum,
a serene space filled with art and history.

Evening:
Return to Assi Ghat for a sunset boat ride or watch the evening aarti.
Dinner:
Enjoy a rustic Banarasi meal at Baati Chokha Restaurant or dine at Tandoor Villa for North Indian
classics.
Day 3
Morning:
Take a 30-minute drive to Sarnath, where Buddha gave his first sermon.
Visit Dhamek Stupa, Archaeological Museum, and Mulagandha Kuti Vihar — spend a few
peaceful hours here.
Lunch:
Return to Varanasi and stop at Lotus Lounge Café by the river.
Afternoon:
Visit a silk weaving workshop in Madanpura or Raj Ghat to meet artisans and buy authentic
Banarasi sarees directly.
If visiting during Dev Deepawali (November), witness the ghats illuminated by thousands of diyas —
best viewed from a boat.
Otherwise, relax by the river and watch life unfold along the Ganga.
Dinner:
End your trip at Bana Lassi Café or Tandoor Villa, soaking in your last night by the river.
Must-Visit Places for Food
Street Food Classics:
Kashi Chat Bhandar – legendary chaat spot
Blue Lassi Shop – dozens of lassi flavours in clay cups
Deena Chaat Bhandar – golgappas & tamatar chaat
Shree Café – vegetarian snacks & coffee
Cafés & Restaurants:
Brown Bread Bakery – rooftop café & organic menu
Open Hand Café – artisan vibe with great coffee
Dolphin Restaurant – river-view dining
